
# Nanu's Eid Shemai

## Ingredients
- 200g vermicelli
- 1 litre milk
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 3 cardamom pods
- 2 tablespoons ghee
- Raisins and nuts

## Instructions
1. Heat ghee in a pan.
2. Roast vermicelli until golden.
3. Boil milk with sugar and cardamom.
4. Add roasted vermicelli.
5. Cook until soft.
6. Garnish with raisins and nuts.
