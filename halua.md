
# Nanu's Suji Halua Recipe

## Ingredients
- 1 cup semolina
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 2 tablespoons ghee
- 1 cup water

## Instructions
1. Heat ghee in a pan.
2. Add semolina and roast until golden.
3. Add sugar and water.
4. Add milk and stir continuously.
5. Cook until the mixture becomes thick.
6. Garnish with nuts and serve warm.
