
# Mitu's Chattogram Mezban Recipe

## Ingredients
- 1 kg beef
- 3 onions
- 2 tablespoons ginger paste
- 2 tablespoons garlic paste
- 2 tablespoons red chilli powder
- 1 teaspoon turmeric
- Mezban spice mix
- Salt and cooking oil

## Instructions
1. Wash and cut the beef into pieces.
2. Heat oil and fry the onions.
3. Add ginger, garlic and spices.
4. Add beef and cook until the spices combine.
5. Add water and simmer until the meat becomes tender.
6. Serve hot with rice.


## Serving Suggestion

Serve Mezban hot with plain rice, salad and lemon.
