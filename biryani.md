
# Nanu's Eid Biryani

## Ingredients
- 2 cups basmati rice
- 500g chicken
- 2 onions
- 1 cup yogurt
- 4 cardamom pods
- 2 cinnamon sticks
- Salt and spices

## Instructions
1. Wash and soak the rice.
2. Marinate chicken with yogurt and spices.
3. Fry onions until golden brown.
4. Cook chicken with spices.
5. Add rice and cook on low heat.
6. Serve hot with salad.
